Our Daily Journey — Small Class, Big Heart 🌱

At BBK we nurture curiosity, kindness, and independence in a cozy, secure environment. With a consistent class of up to 4 children (ages 3–6), each child receives focused attention and tailored learning.

Daily Activity Schedule

Times are approximate and adjusted to the group's needs. Emojis show quick cues for each part of the day.

7:00–8:00

Arrival & Free Play ☀️

Gentle greetings, unpacking, choice-based play (blocks, sensory bins) to ease into the day.

8:00–9:00

Breakfast & Circle Time 🍓📚

Family-style snack or breakfast; morning song, calendar chat, and language-rich story time.

9:00–10:00

Outdoor Play & Gross Motor 🌳

Fresh air, climbing, obstacle play, and cooperative games to build confidence and coordination.

10:00–11:00

Creative Arts 🎨

Open-ended art, music, and sensory exploration — process-focused activities to encourage expression.

11:00–11:45

Learning Centers & Social Play 🧩

Small rotating centers emphasizing early literacy, math concepts, and pretend play for social skills.

11:45–12:30

Lunch & Quiet Social Time 🍽️

Family-style dining, conversation, and supportive feeding for developing independence.

12:30–2:30

Nap / Rest Time 😴

Quiet, dim environment with calming music. Supervised rest with optional quiet activities for non-nappers.

2:30–3:30

Snack & Story Time 📖

Light snack and engaging read-alouds; language development through questions and puppetry.

3:30–4:30

STEM & Sensory 🔬

Simple experiments, building, and sensory tables to spark curiosity and problem-solving.

4:30–5:30

Movement & Music 🎶

Dance, instrument play, and circle games to end the day on a joyful note.

5:30–6:00

Wind-down & Pick-up 🌇

Sharing moments, quick updates to caregivers, and smooth handoffs to families.
